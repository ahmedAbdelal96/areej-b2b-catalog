# Areej B2B Catalog Website — Updated Client Presentation Narrative

## 1. Main Message to Client

The current Areej website introduces the company and its market presence, but the new website should become a practical commercial gateway that helps business visitors reach the right products faster.

The proposed direction is:

> Transform Areej from a simple corporate website into a **Global B2B Catalog Website** that presents Areej’s product families, sister brands, market reach, and request-quote journey clearly in Arabic and English.

This is not a redesign only. It is a product-led business presentation that can help Areej receive better enquiries from companies, distributors, importers, and commercial buyers.

---

## 2. What We Learned From the Current Areej Website

The current website communicates important business signals:

- Areej Al Alam operates across **Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Malaysia**.
- The company has operated since **2006**.
- The business focuses on **importing and marketing consumer products**.
- The company imports goods from the Egyptian market and markets them in Saudi and Malaysian markets.
- Areej has built brands in the Saudi market, including:
  - **Areej Al Alam Tourism**.
  - **Depalma** for consumer goods: perfumes, cosmetics, and consumer products.
  - **Depalma** for furniture and decor.

These signals should become part of the new website’s story and data structure.

---

## 3. Correct Positioning

The new website should not look like a small static company profile.

It should position Areej as:

> A global import and marketing partner with selected consumer product families, Saudi/GCC market experience, and a clear B2B catalogue for business enquiries.

The site should make visitors understand within seconds:

1. What Areej does.
2. Which product sectors it works in.
3. Which markets it operates across.
4. Which brands are connected to the group.
5. How to request product information or a quotation.

---

## 4. Why a B2B Catalog Website Is the Right Model

Areej’s business model is not normal retail checkout.

Business buyers usually need to:

- Browse product categories.
- View product families and specifications.
- Ask about availability, quantities, and supply terms.
- Request quotation or commercial follow-up.
- Communicate with the sales team.

Therefore, the correct model is:

```text
B2B Product Catalog + Request Quote + Contact Sales
```

not:

```text
Retail Cart + Online Checkout
```

---

## 5. Product Families for the Demo

Because the current public website does not show a detailed SKU-level product list, the v0/Vercel prototype should use **representative mock data** inspired by Areej’s visible business areas.

The demo product families should be:

1. **Perfumes & Fragrance Products**
   - Personal perfumes.
   - Body sprays.
   - Fragrance gift sets.
   - Air freshener products.

2. **Cosmetics & Personal Care**
   - Hair care products.
   - Skin care products.
   - Body care products.
   - Beauty retail packs.

3. **Consumer Goods**
   - Household consumer items.
   - Hygiene and care products.
   - Retail-ready packs.
   - Private label opportunities.

4. **Furniture & Decor**
   - Modern seating.
   - Home decor pieces.
   - Hotel/apartment furnishing.
   - Office and commercial furniture.

5. **Brand Development & Market Entry**
   - Not a product category, but a service area.
   - Used to explain Areej’s ability to build brands in the Saudi market.

6. **Tourism Brand / Group Presence**
   - Areej Al Alam Tourism should appear as a sister brand / group achievement, not as a product category inside the product catalogue.

---

## 6. What the New Website Will Achieve

The new website will:

1. Present Areej as an established international business group.
2. Make products and product families visible from the first screen.
3. Support Arabic and English visitors with correct RTL/LTR layout.
4. Help companies request quotations easily.
5. Show Areej’s markets: Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Malaysia.
6. Build credibility through group brands, history, markets, and product focus.
7. Allow future expansion into a CMS-powered B2B portal.

---

## 7. Required Demo Strategy

Before discussing full production, create a premium demo on Vercel showing the business value.

The demo must include:

- Product-first homepage.
- Strong product catalogue.
- Category browsing.
- Product details.
- Quote basket / inquiry flow.
- Request quote page.
- Arabic/English switcher.
- Realistic mock data based on Areej’s visible business areas.
- A section for Areej’s group brands and Saudi market success.

The demo should feel like an international B2B catalogue, not a landing page.

---

## 8. Suggested Client Explanation

Use this explanation when presenting the demo:

> We noticed that Areej’s current website explains the company, but it does not yet help business buyers discover products quickly. The new direction turns the website into a Global B2B Catalog Website, where visitors can browse consumer products, perfumes, cosmetics, furniture and decor categories, view product information, and request quotations. We also highlight Areej’s market presence across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Malaysia, and show the group’s successful brands such as Depalma and Areej Al Alam Tourism as trust signals.

---

## 9. Closing Statement

This project should be sold as a commercial digital catalogue for business growth.

The goal is not just to make Areej look better online. The goal is to make the website help Areej receive better B2B enquiries, present its product families more professionally, and prepare for future expansion into a full product management and quote management platform.
