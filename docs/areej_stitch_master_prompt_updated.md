# Stitch Master Prompt — Areej Global B2B Catalog Website — Updated

Use this prompt to generate/refine the visual design before v0 implementation.

---

## Design Task

Design a premium, product-first, bilingual Arabic/English **Global B2B Catalog Website** for **Areej Al Alam for Import & Marketing**.

This is not a normal company website and not a retail ecommerce store.

It should feel like an international B2B catalog where business buyers can browse product families, view product details, add products to a quote basket, and request a quotation.

---

## Business Context

Areej Al Alam works in importing and marketing consumer products across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Malaysia.

Visible current website content shows that Areej has built brands in the Saudi market, including:

- Areej Al Alam Tourism.
- Depalma for consumer goods, perfumes, cosmetics, and consumer products.
- Depalma for furniture and decor.

The design must use these business areas as the basis for the product catalogue and brand sections.

Do not design a catalogue around unrelated products such as steel, wheat, industrial minerals, or heavy equipment.

---

## Required Visual Direction

Premium Saudi/GCC B2B style:

- White surfaces.
- Deep navy corporate base.
- Warm gold accents.
- Small Depalma red/blue accents only in brand cards and badges.
- Clean product cards.
- Strong business spacing.
- Professional catalogue layout.
- Mobile-first catalog browsing.
- Arabic RTL and English LTR visual awareness.

---

## Product Families to Show

Design product categories for:

1. Perfumes & Fragrance.
2. Cosmetics & Personal Care.
3. Consumer Goods.
4. Furniture & Decor.
5. Packaging & Retail Packs.

---

## Required Screens

Create high-polish screens for:

1. Product-first homepage.
2. Product catalog page.
3. Category landing page.
4. Product detail page.
5. Quote basket / request quote flow.
6. Brands / group achievements page or section.
7. Mobile catalog browsing screen.

---

## Homepage Requirements

The homepage must include:

- Header with logo, nav, language switcher, quote basket, and Request Quote CTA.
- Large hero with product search above the fold.
- Direct category shortcuts.
- Trust stats: Since 2006, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Malaysia, Import & Marketing.
- Featured products.
- How the quote process works.
- Areej Group Brands & Market Success.
- Markets section.
- Services section.
- Final CTA.

Hero English:

```text
Global B2B Product Catalog for Import & Market Expansion
Consumer products, perfumes, cosmetics, furniture, and decor solutions prepared for regional business growth across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Malaysia.
```

Hero Arabic:

```text
كتالوج منتجات B2B عالمي للاستيراد والتوسع في الأسواق
منتجات استهلاكية، عطور، مستحضرات تجميل، أثاث وديكور جاهزة للنمو التجاري في السعودية ومصر وماليزيا.
```

---

## Product Catalog Requirements

The product catalog page must include:

- Search input.
- Category chips.
- Sidebar filters.
- Brand filter.
- Origin filter.
- Packaging filter.
- Market filter.
- Product count and sorting.
- Product grid with at least 12 visible product cards.
- Product cards showing:
  - product image.
  - brand badge.
  - category badge.
  - product name.
  - origin.
  - packaging.
  - MOQ.
  - market badges.
  - View Details CTA.
  - Add to Quote CTA.

---

## Product Detail Requirements

The product detail page must include:

- Breadcrumbs.
- Product image gallery.
- Product name and brand.
- Origin, packaging, MOQ, markets.
- Add to Quote Basket as primary CTA.
- Product specs table.
- Packaging & supply information.
- Related products.
- Right-side RFQ panel.

---

## Quote Basket Requirements

Design the quote basket as a B2B inquiry list, not as ecommerce checkout.

Required elements:

- Header quote basket icon with count.
- Drawer/page with selected products.
- Quantity fields.
- Destination market selector.
- Contact details form.
- Submit request CTA.
- Success state.

Use labels:

- Add to Quote.
- Quote Basket.
- Inquiry List.
- Request Quotation.

Avoid:

- Buy Now.
- Checkout.
- Payment.
- Cart total.

---

## Brand Section

Create a polished section called:

```text
Areej Group Brands & Market Success
```

Cards:

1. Areej Al Alam Tourism.
2. Depalma Consumer Goods — perfumes, cosmetics, consumer products.
3. Depalma Furniture & Decor — furniture and decor.

The brand section should build trust and connect Depalma cards to relevant product categories.

---

## Mobile Requirements

Create mobile screens that show:

- Sticky compact header.
- Catalog search.
- Category chips.
- Product cards.
- Filter drawer.
- Add to Quote button.
- Quote basket indicator.

---

## Final Design Quality

The output should look like a real international B2B catalog website that can be shown to a company decision maker.

It must clearly say visually:

> Areej is an established import and marketing group with product families, successful brands, and a serious B2B quote journey.
